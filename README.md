# ResearchOS

## An Autonomous AI Research Engineer

ResearchOS is a research workflow automation framework designed to support complex knowledge creation tasks.

The system decomposes research objectives into specialized workflows, including literature exploration, theoretical modeling, computational validation, and critical review.

## Motivation

Modern research requires integration of multiple activities:

- discovering relevant literature
- identifying research opportunities
- developing conceptual models
- validating analytical results
- communicating findings

ResearchOS explores how intelligent workflow orchestration can improve the research process.

## Core Capabilities

### Literature Exploration
Organizes related studies and identifies potential research directions.

### Model Development
Transforms research questions into structured analytical frameworks.

### Computational Validation
Supports simulation, numerical experiments, and result verification.

### Critical Review
Evaluates research outputs and highlights potential weaknesses.
